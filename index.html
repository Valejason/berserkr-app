EATING OUT
4-step order (~800 cal, 50 g+ protein, replaces dinner + snack)
  1. Protein first: fish, shrimp or lean steak — grilled, blackened, broiled, seared. Skip fried, breaded, crispy, smothered. Sirloin/filet/flank over ribeye.
  2. One carb: baked potato, rice or 2–3 tortillas. Bread, chips and fries count as your carb.
  3. Double the veg: swap fries or a second side for vegetables or a side salad.
  4. Hidden fat: sauces and dressing on the side. Ask for light butter.
  • Steakhouse: 8–9 oz sirloin/filet, plain baked potato, steamed veg
  • Seafood: grilled salmon or shrimp skewers, rice, veg, no tartar
  • Mexican: steak/shrimp fajitas, 2–3 tortillas, pico, beans; no chip refills, cheese, sour cream
  • Poke: greens base, double shrimp + crab, ponzu
  • Burger: single patty, lettuce wrap or one bun, side salad
  • Italian: grilled fish/steak + veg; pasta = half, box the rest
Drinks: water, sparkling, diet soda. Alcohol max 2 (light beer / spirit + soda) and it replaces the carb. Next meal, back on plan — no making up for it.
